Troubleshooting

Don't panic. Most issues are fixable in under a minute.

Stream dropped

EventLive supports pause and resume to the same link. Tap the go-live button again. Viewers see a brief interruption but the stream resumes at the same URL.

No audio from lav mics

Unplug and replug the USB-C receiver. If still nothing, check that both transmitters are turned on (solid LED). As a last resort, the phone's built-in mic will pick up general ceremony sound.

Phone overheating

Reduce brightness to minimum. Make sure the phone is in shade under the pergola. If it shuts down, let it cool for 2 minutes then reopen EventLive and resume.

Battery dying

Plug in the power bank. This only works if the USB-C port is free — if the Jubolion receiver is using it, you cannot charge during the stream. Start at 100%.

EventLive app crashes

Reopen the app and tap Go Live again. The event link stays the same. If repeated crashes, open the event link in Chrome as a last resort — the browser viewer still works.
